CIR

Research on the training compute needed to reach a given capability.

CIR is DotrixAI's first major research program. It compared candidate systems with strong, optimized Transformers on training compute. Its primary lane was paused on 15 October 2026.

Now · Direction change

Why the primary lane is paused.

On 15 October 2026 the owner paused CIR's primary lane. The target was not reached. The charter is unchanged, and the long term objective is an open decision.

  • 01The fair bar is a strong published recipe

    At 20M parameters, a published recipe needs about 0.58× the time of plain Transformers. Much of an apparent win is already inside it.

  • 02Our advantage came from published parts

    Every part of our best system is published. Against opponents with the same parts, our laptop ratio rose to 0.26 to 0.54. Against plain Transformers it had looked like 0.04 to 0.06.

  • 03What remained faded with scale

    At the first GPU scale point, the counted edge was about 1.0 on web text. Copying carried what was left.

  • 04No original lever

    Five first principles cycles found nothing new. The search stopped by a rule written in advance.

  • 05FLOPs are not time

    Published levers, a hybrid and an expert mixture saved some FLOPs. None saved wall clock time with our kernels.

  • 06The open routes need far more compute

    Reported gains of hybrids and expert mixtures appear at far larger scales. Testing them fairly is beyond our resources.

  • 07The record itself has value

    Most experiments were preregistered, measured and audited. That record now becomes research tasks for AI research agents.

Exact outcomes from 10 to 15 October are withheld for now. They are answer keys of unpublished research tasks. The full account, with evidence IDs, is in the CIR repository.

01 · Research objective

Reach the same capability with substantially less training compute.

minimize  C(CIR, Q) / C(strongest Transformer, Q)

C is the training compute needed to reach capability Q. Both systems must reach the same level before costs are compared.

The owner clarified this objective on 12 October 2026. Training compute is primary. Inference, memory and energy are reported separately.

The comparator is the strongest optimized Transformer system. It may use every transferable published technique.

Transformers are allowed, and novelty is optional. Before 12 October we reported total cost against two weaker denominators.

What training compute includes

  • Pretraining and any repeated or staged runs
  • Auxiliary training, such as gates and memories
  • Building tables and indexes from the data
  • Necessary preprocessing
  • Measured time on named hardware, next to counted FLOPs

Denominators

  • Primary. The strongest optimized Transformer system for the same capability.
  • Partial. An opponent given selected matching parts, to explain a result.
  • Plain. A standard Transformer. Diagnostic only since 12 October.
{{tagMeasured}}
Read directly from an instrument, such as BPB or time per update.
{{tagEstimated}}
Computed from measurements, such as a cost ratio at matched Q.
{{tagProjected}}
Extrapolated beyond what was run. Never mixed with the other two.

03 · Current research state

What is being compared, and on what.

Implementation details beyond this level stay private.

  • Program phase

    Primary lane paused since 15 October 2026. No experiment is running or queued.

  • Best laptop system

    A051. A small one attention Transformer with a hashed n gram input memory. Also counted n gram tables, a cache, a pointer and a learned gate.

  • Prior art

    Every part and their combination are published, including modded nanogpt PR 380.

  • Strong recipe on the GPU

    A published 20M recipe. A dense core plus a hashed n gram input memory. Also value embeddings, skips and a softcap.

  • GPU phase

    One rented A100. Dense models of 4.7M to 85M parameters. One epoch of 0.38B tokens, one seed.

  • Laptop phase

    One laptop CPU, Intel Core i5 13420H. Networks up to about 3M parameters, CIR mixers up to 7M.

  • Data

    English and Indonesian. Wikipedia text on the laptop, web text on the GPU.

  • Quality metric

    Bits per token on unseen contexts. On the GPU also bits per byte, with BLiMP as a grammar check.

  • Strong recipe against plain Transformers

    About 0.58× the time and 0.43× the FLOPs at matched quality, at 20M. {{tagEstimated}}

  • Best laptop system against partial opponents

    0.26 to 0.54× at up to 4M parameters. Against plain Transformers 0.038 to 0.055×. {{tagEstimated}}

  • Counted organization on the GPU

    At 4.7M to 18.9M parameters, about 1.0 on web text. On Wikipedia text 0.51 to 0.57. {{tagEstimated}}

  • Candidates against strong recipes

    None reached 0.75 at 20M or 85M. Exact values are withheld for now.

04 · Current claim

Current claim

Scope: one laptop CPU up to about 7M parameters, one A100 up to 85M. Snapshot of {{cirAsOf}}.

CIR did not reach its primary target. No candidate beat a strong optimized recipe by the signal gate.

Our large small scale ratios came from published parts, measured against weaker opponents.

Against opponents with the same parts, the best laptop system sits at 0.26 to 0.54×.

At the first GPU scale point, the counted edge was about 1.0 on web text.

No original training compute lever was found. The primary lane is paused.

06 · Cost to capability

A curve, not a headline number.

An advantage at one quality level, budget or language can vanish at another. We report each separately.

TotalCostRatio(Q) ≈ ρ_u × ρ_c

  • ρ_u

    Relative updates, or tokens, needed to reach matched Q.

  • ρ_c

    Relative cost per update, measured on one canonical instrument.

  • Counted statistics

    Building the tables costs about 0.65% of training time, measured. We first stated 0.1 to 0.2%.

  • Time and FLOPs

    On the GPU we report measured time next to counted FLOPs. FLOP savings did not survive in time.

08 · Reduced uncertainty

What We Have Ruled Out

Each item below closed a branch of the search. That makes the next experiment cheaper to choose.

Architecture

Pure recurrence under a strong optimizer

A delta rule model loses to a Muon trained Transformer at this scale.

Architecture

Longer context as the route to 0.50

Moving from 4,096 to 8,192 tokens made A010's per token ratio worse.

Architecture

Static lookup as recall

Lookup tables cannot perform in context associative recall. A softmax layer is still needed.

Architecture

Several hybrid variants

Local selection, a surprisal cache and a per head hybrid layer each failed their gates.

Architecture

Recurrent state replacing distant attention

When attention was windowed, A010 lost as much quality as the Transformer. Its state carried almost no distant copies.

Analysis

Mixer substitution reaching 0.50 here

Replace attention with a free recurrent core. Canonical timings still leave a ratio of about 0.77 to 0.87. {{tagEstimated}}

Architecture

Thin recurrent hybrids against B1A

A025 kept part of A010's learning advantage but only broke even on cost.

Analysis

Training rules specific to recurrence

Every training rule lever found also applies to Transformers. None is specific to CIR here.

Capability

Reliable state tracking from sparse data

With 6% parity examples, the capability emerged in only one of three runs.

Capability

Cheap state tracking

A one layer Transformer with written steps reached the permutation target more cheaply.

Learning

Formal language warm up at 4M

A short warm up before text raised BPB. It hurt the CIR hybrid twice as much.

Organization

Counts inside the training loss

Training the network jointly with the counts failed. Mixing at inference works.

Organization

One gate for every language

A gate tuned on one language was worse than fixed weights on the other.

Organization

Smaller networks, pruned tables, first layer attention

Below 0.82M parameters, pruning rare counts or moving attention first all made results worse.

Method

Averages across languages

An average hid a large English gap. Claims are now reported per language.

Method

Ranking architectures at 250 updates

Rank correlation with final results was 0.09. Discovery runs now last 750 updates.

Method

Uncontrolled timing

Sessions with unpinned cores or background load are quarantined and remeasured.

Method

Endpoint cost for capability

Charging a plateaued baseline its full budget inflated CIR's advantage. It was corrected.

Scale

Scaling the counted organization

At the first GPU scale point its edge was about 1.0 on web text. It was not scaled further.

Learning

Count and copy channels as training levers

They sped up early training on a strong recipe. Then they faded before a fifth of an epoch.

Search

An original lever at laptop scale

Five first principles cycles found only rediscoveries. The search stopped by its own rule.

Architecture

Published shifts at 20M and 85M

Cheap levers, a gated delta hybrid and an expert mixture stayed far from 0.75.

Method

Evaluation sets with different relations

A language gap was partly an evaluation effect. Set provenance is now recorded for every claim.

Method

FLOP counts as cost

FLOP savings did not survive in measured time. Claims now report both.

09 · Capability evaluation

Lower BPB is not general intelligence.

BPB measures text compression. Models with equal BPB can differ in recall, copying and state tracking. At 4M parameters, many capabilities cannot be measured at all.

10 · Scaling status

Small scale results do not prove large scale behavior.

Laptop networks stayed below about 7M parameters. GPU runs reached 85M for one epoch. Evidence, extrapolation and hypothesis are kept apart.

Evidence {{tagEstimated}}

First GPU scale point

At 4.7M to 18.9M parameters, the counted edge was about 1.0 on web text. Copying carried the rest.

Evidence

Published shifts, 20M to 85M

A gated delta hybrid and an expert mixture gave small FLOP gains. The hybrid's edge shrank from 20M to 85M.

Evidence {{tagEstimated}}

Budget ladder, laptop

Against B1A at equal budget, the counted average stays at 0.048 to 0.067×. That holds from 0.5× to 4×. Indonesian stays near 0.04×.

Confound, resolved

English and data repetition

English faded as its small corpus repeated. With fresh English text it behaved like Indonesian.

Extrapolation {{tagProjected}}

A fitted multiplier

A loss curve fit suggests about 15× equivalent compute. Part of it reflects the fixed size opponent's limit.

Evidence {{tagEstimated}}

CIR mixers across widths

Against B1A, A010's update advantage stayed at 0.70 to 0.71 from 4M to 7M. Its cost stayed above 1.

Unknown

Not yet tested

Models above 85M, more seeds, longer training, fine grained expert mixtures with efficient kernels. These exceed our resources.

14 · Methodology

How CIR evaluates a candidate.

Hypotheses

Hypothesis led, with credences

Each experiment tests a named hypothesis. Beliefs are updated before and after.

Criteria

Frozen success and failure criteria

Pass conditions and predictions are written down before any result is seen.

Funnel

Discovery separate from confirmation

Discovery runs are short. Confirmation needs a full run, a second seed and a baseline attack.

Baselines

Adversarial baseline strengthening

A dedicated lane tries to make the Transformer beat CIR.

Mechanism

Mechanism before optimization

Probes explain why a result happens before it is built upon.

Fidelity

Multi fidelity experiments

Analysis and evaluation only probes come first. Full training runs come last.

Cost

Canonical cost accounting

Fixed cores, calibration and validity checks. Invalid sessions are remeasured.

Capability

Capability probes beyond BPB

Copying, recall, bracket closing and position use are checked on the same checkpoints.

Search

First principles architecture search

Candidates are derived from bounds and primitives before literature is consulted.

Prior art

Adversarial prior art review

Every idea is searched against published work. Rediscoveries are labeled as such.

Red team

Periodic knowledge red teaming

Accepted conclusions are attacked on a schedule. Errors found are logged and corrected.

Status

Epistemic labels

Knowledge carries a status, such as supported, contested, superseded or quarantined.

Charter

Every session binds to the charter

Since 12 October each session reads the charter and method in full. A tool checks it.

Stop rule

Search with a stop rule

First principles search stops after two empty cycles. The rule fired on 13 October.

15 · Limitations

What these results cannot tell you.

  • Model scale is small: about 7M parameters on the laptop and 85M on one GPU.

  • Hardware is one laptop CPU and one rented A100. GPU runs use one seed.

  • Exact outcomes from 10 to 15 October are withheld for now.

  • Quality is bits per token or per byte, in English and Indonesian only.

  • Grammar, binding, reasoning and generation were not measurable on the laptop.

  • No complete optimized opponent was built at any scale. The opponents used are partial.

  • Count tables need hundreds of megabytes, about 434 MB on the laptop.

  • Methods overlap with prior art, including modded nanogpt, DeltaNet, RWKV 7 and Engram.

  • Results can change as baselines improve. This has already happened several times.

  • On the GPU, FLOP savings did not carry over into measured time.

16 · Publication and reproducibility

This is not a promise of full open source. Some implementation details will remain proprietary.
